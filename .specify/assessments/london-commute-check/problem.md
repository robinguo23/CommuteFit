# Problem Definition / 问题定义：伦敦求职通勤评估工具

- **Slug**: london-commute-check
- **Created / 日期**: 2026-10-26
- **Inputs used / 使用输入**: intake.md, research.md

## Problem Statement / 问题陈述

The idea owner, a recently arrived London job seeker urgently seeking part-time income, must repeatedly pre-screen roles on chain-brand career sites to decide whether the commute from home is reachable and affordable. The current process requires separate checks for earliest arrival, latest return and Sunday service, plus manual work to understand complete fares, transfers, discounts and caps; this is a firsthand case, while wider prevalence remains unknown.

提出者本人刚到伦敦、急需兼职收入，在连锁品牌官网逐个筛选岗位时，必须反复预先判断从家出发是否能按班次到达、费用是否负担得起。当前流程需要分别检查最早到岗、最晚返程和周日服务，还要手动核算完整票价、换乘、折扣和封顶；这是一个亲身案例，其他人群中的普遍程度仍未知。

## Affected Users & Stakeholders / 受影响用户与相关方

- **Users / 用户**: The idea owner is the sole intended user of the MVP. Open-source reuse is a possible future direction, not a current requirement. / MVP 只服务提出者本人；未来可能开源供他人复用，但不属于当前范围。
- **Stakeholders / 相关方**: The idea owner/job seeker; any authoritative transport and fare-data providers if the idea proceeds. / 提出者/求职者；若推进，还涉及权威交通与票价数据提供方。

## Goals / 目标

- Reduce the idea owner’s repeated manual pre-application checks across job listings. / 减少提出者逐个岗位投递前反复手动查询。
- Use a postcode as the home origin; let the user set their acceptable commute-time limit. / 使用 postcode 作为出发地；通勤时间上限由用户自行设置。
- Make earliest arrival, latest return and Sunday/other relevant service availability clear for the user's availability. / 清楚呈现求职者可工作时间内的最早到岗、最晚返程及周日等相关服务。
- Make travel costs, transfers, potentially relevant discounts and caps understandable, while clearly distinguishing confirmed amounts from eligibility or fare details that the available evidence cannot establish. / 清楚说明交通费用、换乘、可能相关的优惠和封顶，并区分已确认的金额与现有依据无法确认的资格或票价细节。
- Explain the evidence behind whether a role appears reachable and affordable, rather than assert an unexplained universal score. / 解释职位为何看起来可达且可负担，而非给出没有说明的统一分数。

## Non-Goals / 非目标

- Decide whether a job is worthwhile overall based on salary, duties, culture, or career prospects.
- Find jobs or integrate with individual chain-brand career sites in the initial concept.
- Replace a general-purpose journey planner.
- Claim exact fare, discount or service availability when authoritative data does not support it.
- Predict real-time punctuality or guarantee arrival; assess commute practicality from scheduled public-transport data only.
- 不综合薪酬、职责、文化或职业前景判断职位整体价值。
- 初始概念不负责找工作或接入各连锁品牌招聘网站。
- 不取代通用路线规划器。
- 权威数据不支持时，不声称票价、优惠或班次一定准确可用。
- 不预测实时准点或保证到达；只依据计划公共交通数据评估通勤可行性。

## Cost of Inaction / 不解决的代价

For the idea owner, the current cost is repeated mental effort: separate route searches at shift boundaries and on Sundays, plus manual fare research, before deciding whether to apply. Potentially rejecting viable jobs or spending time pursuing unaffordable ones is plausible but not yet observed or quantified. Wider impact is unknown.

对提出者而言，当前代价是反复耗费心力：投递前要分开查询班次边界和周日路线，还要手动查票价。错过实际可行的工作，或把时间花在负担不起的岗位上，都是可能后果，但尚未观察或量化；更广泛影响未知。
