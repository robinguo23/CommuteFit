在 69 条返回行程中，66 条含票价数据，3 条没有 `fare` 对象。对本批次同时含 `totalCost` 和 `fares[]` 的 66 条，`fares[]` 中 `cost` 的合计均与 `totalCost` 相同。这是本批次内的对照结果，不构成对所有路线的保证。

### 2. FareTo coverage and amount representation / FareTo 覆盖范围和金额表示

- Charing Cross → Baker Street returned adult cash £7.00, PAYG off-peak £3.00 and PAYG peak £3.10. Journey Planner returned integer costs `300`/`310` for matching cases. Hammersmith and Highbury route comparisons also matched the FareTo route rows in the tested examples. This supports interpreting those Journey Planner integers as pence for these cases; do not assume that for an unfamiliar field without checking its contract and fare context.
- The tested bus-stop pair and St Pancras Rail → Liverpool Street Rail each returned an empty FareTo array (`[]`) with HTTP 200. That means no fare quote was returned; it does not mean the fare is £0.
- National Rail/Elizabeth line Journey Planner options in this test had no fare object. Treat the fare as unknown unless a supported authoritative source provides it.
- For the 2 tested station-pair queries that returned alternative FareTo routes, the response contained multiple route rows. The FareTo result is a station-pair fare reference and must not be added to Journey Planner components unless the scopes are proven disjoint.

- Charing Cross 至 Baker Street 返回成人现金票 £7.00、PAYG 非高峰 £3.00 和 PAYG 高峰 £3.10。匹配案例中的 Journey Planner 返回整数 `300`/`310`。Hammersmith 和 Highbury 的已测路线票价也与对应 FareTo 票价行相符。这支持在这些案例中把 Journey Planner 整数理解为便士；遇到陌生字段仍应先核对接口定义及票价语境。
- 测试的公交站点对和 St Pancras Rail 至 Liverpool Street Rail 的 FareTo 均在 HTTP 200 下返回空数组 `[]`。这表示没有返回报价，不表示票价为 £0。
- 本批次的 National Rail/Elizabeth line 规划路线没有 `fare` 对象。除非有受支持的权威来源提供金额，否则应保持为未知。
- 两个返回替代路线的站对 FareTo 查询都包含多条路线票价行。FareTo 是站对票价参考；除非已证明收费范围互不重叠，否则不得把它与 Journey Planner 的票价项相加。

### 3. A real bus-fare change is visible across the tested dates / 测试日期跨越公交票价变更

The bus-only route returned `totalCost=175` for the late Saturday 31 October query, `185` for Sunday 1 November at 23:00, and `185` for Monday 2 November at 00:30. TfL's official fares page lists the adult bus/tram PAYG fare as £1.75 before the change and £1.85 from 1 November 2026; the Mayor's decision states the freeze ends at **04:30 on 1 November**. The observed journey results align with that effective date. This batch did not query exactly 04:29 and 04:30, so it did not independently verify the minute boundary.

公交路线在 10 月 31 日周六晚班查询返回 `totalCost=175`，11 月 1 日周日 23:00 返回 `185`，11 月 2 日周一 00:30 也返回 `185`。TfL 官方票价页面列出变更前成人公交/电车 PAYG 为 £1.75、2026 年 11 月 1 日起为 £1.85；市长决定说明冻结在 11 月 1 日 **04:30** 结束。观测到的规划结果与生效日期一致。本批次没有恰好查询 04:29 和 04:30，因此尚未独立验证分钟边界。

Sources / 来源: [TfL bus and tram fares](https://tfl.gov.uk/fares/find-fares/bus-and-tram-fares); [Mayor's decision on July 2026 fare changes](https://www.london.gov.uk/md3517-july-2026-fare-changes).

### 4. Peak-boundary responses need caution / 高峰边界响应需要谨慎处理

The weekday test returned unexpected fare labels near the published peak windows. For example, journeys with first tap timestamps 06:30 and 06:34 were labelled `Off Peak`, while 06:38 was `Peak`; at 16:01 one option was `Off Peak`, while options at 16:05 and 16:09 were `Peak`. At 09:31 and 19:02, the returned items were `Off Peak`. The fares page describes weekday peak periods as 06:30–09:30 and 16:00–19:00, so the 06:30/06:34 and 16:01 observations do not line up simply with the published clock windows.

工作日测试在公开高峰时段边界附近返回了需要解释的票价标签。例如，首个 tap 时间为 06:30 和 06:34 的行程标记为 `Off Peak`，06:38 则为 `Peak`；16:01 的一条方案标记为 `Off Peak`，16:05 和 16:09 的方案则为 `Peak`。09:31 和 19:02 返回的项目为 `Off Peak`。票价页面说明工作日高峰时段为 06:30–09:30 和 16:00–19:00，因此 06:30/06:34 与 16:01 的观测不能简单地用公开时钟区间解释。

Do not recalculate these API labels from the requested query time. Keep TfL's returned fare classification and timestamp visible, and mark edge cases as needing validation. Follow-up should repeat the boundary queries and compare them with the TfL Single Fare Finder for the same route and times before implementing a local peak/off-peak rule.

不要只根据用户查询时间自行重算 API 返回的票价标签。应保留 TfL 返回的票价分类和时间戳，并将边界案例列为待核实。实现本地高峰/非高峰规则前，应重复边界查询，并用相同路线和时间在 TfL Single Fare Finder 对照。

### 5. Weekend early shifts and the clock change / 周末早班与夏令时回拨

- For this central London stop pair, `Arriving` by 02:30 returned Saturday and Sunday night-bus itineraries. The arrive-by queries showed services reaching the destination by about 02:02–02:27 on Saturday and 02:03–02:25 on Sunday. A separate `Departing` at 02:30 query showed later arrivals. This validates the need to query the intended boundary (`Arriving` before a shift; `Departing` after a finish) separately.
- On the clock-change Sunday, querying local 01:30 returned a journey starting at 01:35; fare taps were timestamped with `+00:00`, while Journey Planner display times had no offset. The response corresponds to an offset-aware standard-time tap, but the request format did not let us select the first or second local 01:30 occurrence explicitly.
- The route pair is one illustrative central London test, not proof of service availability for other home postcodes or job sites.

- 对本次伦敦市中心站点对，`Arriving` 02:30 查询在周六和周日均返回夜间公交路线。周六路线约于 02:02–02:27 到达，周日约于 02:03–02:25 到达。单独的 `Departing` 02:30 查询则返回更晚的到达时间。这验证了应分别查询正确的时间边界（班前用 `Arriving`，下班后用 `Departing`）。
- 夏令时回拨周日查询本地 01:30，返回的路线从 01:35 开始；票价 tap 时间带 `+00:00`，Journey Planner 展示的时间则没有时区偏移。响应中的 tap 对应带偏移的冬令时，但请求格式无法明确指定两个本地 01:30 中的第一个或第二个。
- 该站点对只是伦敦市中心的示例，不能证明其他居住 postcode 或工作地点也有相同服务。

## Requirements impact / 对需求的影响

1. Model and display **TfL fare items separately from itinerary legs**. When `fare.totalCost` is present, use it as the provider's route-level adult PAYG fare; show the available `fares[]` items as its breakdown. Do not assign one independent full fare to each vehicle leg.
2. Do not add FareTo station-pair prices on top of Journey Planner fare items unless a rule/source proves the quoted scopes are disjoint. An empty FareTo result or missing Journey Planner fare stays unknown.
3. Treat fare effective dates as first-class data. The 1 November 2026 bus change shows that the route's fare depends on the travel date and even the transport-day boundary.
4. Keep conditional concession displays separate and source-backed. The tested journey request does not identify the user's specific payment device or concession eligibility.
5. Keep daily capping as guidance when the user's complete travel history and same-card/device use are unknown.
6. Keep the user's requested future fare-saving hints as a separate product direction: hints must attach to an actual interchange/leg, state the required action (such as a pink-reader validation), payment assumptions and source, and must not invent a saving amount.

1. 票价模型和界面必须把 **TfL 票价项与路线 leg 分开**。存在 `fare.totalCost` 时，使用该路线级成人 PAYG 总价；同时显示 `fares[]` 返回的票价拆分。不能把每个乘车 leg 都分配一笔独立完整票价。
2. 除非规则/来源证明收费范围互不重叠，否则不得把 FareTo 站对票价叠加到 Journey Planner 票价项上。FareTo 空结果或 Journey Planner 缺少票价时保持未知。
3. 票价生效日期必须作为核心数据。2026 年 11 月 1 日公交票价变更说明金额取决于出行日期及交通日边界。
4. 优惠显示仍须独立且有来源。已测 Journey Planner 请求不包含用户的具体支付设备或优惠资格信息。
5. 用户的完整行程和同卡/设备使用情况未知时，每日封顶仅作说明。
6. 用户提出的后续“省钱提示”仍为独立产品方向：提示须绑定实际换乘点/leg，说明所需操作（例如粉色读卡器验证）、付款假设和来源，不得编造节省金额。
